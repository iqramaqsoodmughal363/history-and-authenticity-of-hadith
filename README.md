## 📚 What is a Hadith?

In Islamic tradition, a **Hadith** refers to the recorded sayings, actions, and tacit approvals of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Alongside the Quran, Hadith forms the foundation of Islamic law, theology, and practice.

## 🔬 The Science of Hadith Authentication

Muslim scholars developed a sophisticated system of criticism to ensure the authenticity of reports. The main criteria for a Hadith to be classified as **Authentic (*Sahih*)** are:

1. **Connected Chain (*Isnad*):** An unbroken chain of narrators going back to the Prophet.
2. **Reliable Narrators:** Each narrator must be known for integrity and trustworthiness.
3. **Precise Memory:** Narrators must have accurate memory and transmission.
4. **No Contradiction:** The text must not contradict other established Hadith.
5. **No Hidden Defects:** Free from subtle flaws that compromise authenticity.
